# 04 — Map and Province Design

## Art direction for geography
Use a stylized fictional country inspired by real-world geographic logic, not a one-to-one copy of a real political map.

This gives:
- original identity;
- easier balance;
- no need to defend historical accuracy;
- cleaner silhouettes.

## MVP map
6 provinces:
- Home North
- Home South
- Central Keep
- Forest March
- River Gate
- Highland Hold

One capital per team.
One central contested castle.

## Castle design
Every castle has:
- 4 readable gates;
- a central courtyard;
- capture ring;
- defender rally area;
- attacker staging area;
- banners;
- resource visual language.

Each gate should look tactically different without requiring unique mechanics.

Examples:
- Gate A: open approach.
- Gate B: narrow bridge.
- Gate C: forest cover.
- Gate D: high-ground approach.

## Province map visualization
When zoomed out:
- province fill color;
- border;
- castle icon;
- income pip;
- attack warning;
- capture animation.

When zoomed in:
- actual world geometry dominates.

## Strategic topology
Prefer 2–3 meaningful routes over a maze.

A province should answer one of:
- income;
- route;
- defense;
- staging;
- victory.

Avoid filler provinces.

## Spawn and travel
Do not place castles far apart simply to make the world “feel large”.
Roblox retention rewards useful density.

## Map expansion later
Map 2 should not just be Map 1 with new textures.
Change topology:
- islands;
- river network;
- mountain choke points;
- coastal ports;
- asymmetric high-value center.
