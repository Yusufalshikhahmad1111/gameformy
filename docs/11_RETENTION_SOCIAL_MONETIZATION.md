# 11 — Retention, Social and Monetization

## Retention loop
Match:
→ earn progression
→ unlock cosmetics/identity
→ party
→ rematch
→ improve skill
→ seasonal goals

## Social
MVP:
- party;
- team chat/pings;
- quick tactical signals.

Later:
- clans;
- clan banners;
- seasonal rankings;
- tournaments.

## Tactical pings
Use:
- Attack
- Defend
- Rally
- Enemy here
- Need help

Pings should be faster than typing.

## Progression
Progression should mostly reward:
- cosmetics;
- banners;
- commander appearances;
- emotes;
- map-room themes;
- profile titles.

Do not sell decisive troop power.

## Monetization
Safe starting model:
- cosmetics;
- battle pass;
- optional private servers;
- profile customization.

Avoid:
- paid damage;
- paid capture speed;
- paid income multipliers in competitive matches.

## Live operations
Season:
- new map;
- new cosmetic set;
- limited event;
- leaderboard reset;
- balance patch.

Never make live ops depend on constant new core systems.
