# Stage 19 — Balance Simulation, Performance & Visual QA
## Objective

Turn balancing and QA into repeatable engineering.

## Simulation

Run:
- army-vs-army;
- formation-vs-formation;
- role counters;
- terrain modifiers;
- economy growth;
- bot-vs-bot;
- comeback scenarios.

## Metrics

Capture:
- time to first command;
- time to first capture;
- first enemy contact;
- average army lifetime;
- retreat rate;
- castle turnover;
- income per minute;
- resource deficit frequency;
- rematch rate;
- tutorial abandonment.

## Visual QA

For every map:
- lobby;
- spawn;
- castle;
- army;
- capture;
- combat;
- map;
- results.

## Device matrix

At minimum:
- desktop;
- landscape mobile;
- narrow mobile.

## Definition of Done

- no dominant troop/formation;
- no runaway economy;
- bots provide pressure without cheating;
- no major FPS collapse;
- no critical UI overlap;
- no critical gameplay regression.
