# Stage 18 — Presentation, Personality, Audio & VFX
## Objective

Make the new systems feel alive.

## Soldier personality

Each soldier gets bounded personality dimensions:
- Courage
- Aggression
- Discipline
- Loyalty

Each dimension:
- 0–100;
- hidden by default;
- lightly influences behavior.

## Morale

Morale increases:
- victories;
- nearby friendly support;
- supplied army;
- successful formation.

Morale decreases:
- casualties;
- losing leader/commander;
- being surrounded;
- isolation;
- lack of supplies.

Low morale:
- lower accuracy;
- slower regroup;
- higher retreat chance.

High morale:
- better cohesion;
- slightly stronger first-contact behavior;
- faster regroup.

Do not allow morale to create wildly unpredictable outcomes.

## Presentation

Battle:
- projectile trails;
- hit flashes;
- banner motion;
- dust;
- subtle impact VFX.

Castle capture:
- gate highlight;
- progress treatment;
- banner transition;
- territory pulse;
- short audio cue.

Victory:
- territory overview;
- army summary;
- rewards;
- rematch.

## Definition of Done

The player can understand battle state by observing the world, even before reading the HUD.
