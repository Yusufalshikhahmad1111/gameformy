# 08 — Art Direction

## Target
**Premium stylized realism / handcrafted medieval diorama.**

Not:
- photorealism;
- low-poly placeholder;
- generic fantasy asset pack;
- AI-art collage.

## Shape language
Large readable silhouettes.
Strong castle landmarks.
Units readable from zoomed-out camera.

## Materials
Limit the material vocabulary:
- stone;
- plaster;
- wood;
- iron;
- cloth;
- earth;
- water.

## Team color
Team color belongs primarily to:
- banners;
- pennants;
- capture VFX;
- UI;
- subtle trim.

Do not recolor entire buildings.

## Castle production rule
Create one hero castle to establish the language.
Then build modular:
- wall;
- gatehouse;
- tower;
- courtyard;
- bridge;
- banner;
- market/yard.

## Unit design
Three base silhouettes:
- spear wall;
- archer line;
- cavalry wedge.

Variants should preserve silhouette.

## Lighting
Use a consistent world lighting profile.
Avoid every object having its own fake glow.

Use:
- directional sunlight;
- soft ambient fill;
- controlled atmospheric depth;
- restrained particles.

## VFX
Capture:
- banner rise;
- territory pulse;
- dust;
- short light sweep.

Combat:
- impact;
- arrows;
- shield/spear reactions;
- dust.

No screen-filling explosions for ordinary attacks.

## UI typography
One display face for titles.
One highly readable UI face.
Arabic fallback must be intentionally selected, not accidental.

## Art QA
Reject an asset if:
- its perspective disagrees with nearby assets;
- material scale is inconsistent;
- silhouette is noisy;
- colors are unmotivated;
- it looks like a random asset-store import.
