# Decision Log

## D001 — Original hybrid instead of clone
Reason: direct competitors cover individual mechanics, but the desired combination can have its own identity.

## D002 — Fictional country
Reason: improves originality and balance while avoiding unnecessary real-world political/geographic constraints.

## D003 — One primary MVP resource
Reason: economy must be understandable before depth is added.

## D004 — Four-gate capture with contested pause
Reason: preserves the requested fantasy while reducing frustrating capture resets.

## D005 — Formation abstraction
Reason: commander fantasy without destroying Roblox server/mobile performance.

## D006 — 10–16 minute target
Reason: enough time for strategy, short enough for replayability.

## D007 — Cosmetics-first monetization
Reason: competitive integrity and long-term trust.
