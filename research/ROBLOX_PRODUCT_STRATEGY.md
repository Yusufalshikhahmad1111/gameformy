# Roblox Product Strategy

## What tends to help discovery
The game must communicate its fantasy visually in thumbnails and the first minute.

Store-page promise:
**Build an army. Capture castles. Command the battlefield.**

Avoid a title/thumbnail that only says “strategy”.

## Thumbnail composition
Hero castle + three visible army types + clear territory color conflict.
One focal point.
Minimal text.

## First-session optimization
The first minute should contain:
- movement;
- command;
- objective;
- enemy sight;
- reward.

Do not make players spend the first five minutes reading menus.

## Replayability
Replayability should come from:
- map topology;
- team composition;
- tactical choices;
- party play;
- evolving territory.

Not from arbitrary random loot.

## Social proof
Encourage:
- party formation;
- tactical pings;
- rematch;
- shareable match result.

## Content cadence
A small polished update is better than a large low-quality update.

## Mobile
Assume a significant portion of Roblox players are touch users.
Design commands as a compact radial/context system.

## Product principle
**If a feature does not improve the next match, the social loop, or the player fantasy, postpone it.**
