# Competitive Research — Design Benchmark

## Direct Roblox benchmarks

### Command An Army
Useful benchmark for:
- team-vs-team warfare;
- recruiting troops;
- capturing points;
- formations;
- upgrades.

Lesson:
The “command an army” fantasy is already legible to Roblox players. Our differentiation must come from the province/castle economy and gate-based territorial warfare.

### Build an Army!
Useful benchmark for:
- resource farming;
- army growth;
- conquest;
- upgrade loop.

Lesson:
Resource generation must create a visible reason to fight over territory.

### Conquer The World WW2
Useful benchmark for:
- territory expansion;
- world-map strategy;
- economy/industry;
- large-scale control.

Lesson:
The strategic map needs a readable macro objective, but our matches should be dramatically shorter and more tactile.

## Genre benchmarks outside Roblox
Use as design references only:
- Conqueror's Blade — formation/siege fantasy.
- Total War — army positioning and battlefield readability.
- Stronghold — castle/economy fantasy.
- Foxhole — logistics/frontline importance.

## Conclusion
The concept is not mechanically unprecedented. That is good.

The product opportunity is the **combination and execution**:
castle gates + province economy + direct formation command + short Roblox-native matches + premium presentation.

## Clone boundary
Replicate:
- generic mechanics;
- genre conventions;
- player-flow lessons.

Do not replicate:
- source code;
- private assets;
- maps;
- logos;
- names;
- exact UI layouts;
- proprietary characters;
- distinctive art.

The goal is a recognizable genre product with an original identity.
