# Roblox Studio QA Prompt

You are the final Studio QA engineer.

Do not change architecture unless required to fix a verified defect.

Test:
1. Fresh server.
2. Two players.
3. Team assignment.
4. Spawn.
5. Army selection.
6. Move.
7. Attack.
8. Castle capture.
9. Contested capture.
10. Territory color update.
11. Economy tick.
12. Victory.
13. Disconnect.
14. Invalid remote calls.
15. Mobile viewport.

For every failure provide:
- exact reproduction;
- output/error;
- root cause;
- fix;
- retest result.

Do not say “works” without observing the runtime.
