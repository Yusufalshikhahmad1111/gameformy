# Playtest Analyst Prompt

Observe 5–10 first-time players without coaching them.

Ask after the match:
- What did you think you were supposed to do?
- What made you attack that castle?
- Did you understand the capture rule?
- Did you know why you gained resources?
- Did formations feel useful?
- Where did you get bored?
- Would you play another match?

Prioritize observed behavior over what players claim they would do.

Convert findings into:
P0 confusion blockers
P1 usability problems
P2 polish
P3 future ideas
